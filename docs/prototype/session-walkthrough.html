Every screen below is drawn from the database as it stands: the lesson, its glosses, the questions and their wrong answers, the ability scale and the item bank. Colours are the app's own tokens.

Figures marked example are illustrative: no learner has answered anything yet, so mastery, streaks and scores have no real values to show. Everything else — the reading text, the glosses, the items, the ability bands, the bank's spread — comes out of content:build-activities and media:measure.
